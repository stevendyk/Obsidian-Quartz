### Augustine - Love God, do as you please

**“Love God and do whatever you please”** is advice from St. Augustine, meaning that when you truly love God, your desires and choices become aligned with God’s will, so you won’t do things that would offend him.

#### Understanding the Quote

“Love God and do whatever you please” is a well-known phrase attributed to St. Augustine. This statement suggests that when a person truly loves God, their desires and actions will naturally align with His will. Therefore, they will not engage in behaviors that would offend God.

#### The Meaning Behind the Quote

- Alignment with God's Will: The essence of Augustine's message is that genuine love for God transforms a person's heart. When you love God, your choices reflect that love, leading you to act in ways that honor Him.
- Freedom in Christ: This quote emphasizes the freedom believers have in Christ. Loving God allows individuals to make choices without the burden of fear, as their desires will increasingly align with what is good and right.

#### Misinterpretations to Avoid

While the quote is liberating, it can be misunderstood. Here are some common misinterpretations:

|Misinterpretation|Clarification|
|---|---|
|It implies a license to sin.|True love for God leads to actions that honor Him, not sinful behavior.|
|It suggests automatic right actions.|Love for God requires ongoing effort and alignment with biblical teachings.|

#### The Role of the Holy Spirit

The transformation of desires is facilitated by the Holy Spirit. As believers grow in their relationship with God, they develop new desires that reflect His character. This process is gradual and requires faith and obedience.

#### Conclusion

In summary, “Love God and do whatever you please” encapsulates the idea that a heart devoted to God will naturally seek to do what pleases Him. This understanding encourages believers to cultivate their love for God, leading to a life that reflects His will and character.
