## Gospel Truths for the Critical Spirit, of Self or Otherwise

[On Being a Self-Critical Christian](https://www.chrismartin.fyi/p/on-being-a-self-critical-christian?publication_id=49806\&post_id=199609769\&isFreemail=true\&triedRedirect=true) _Chris Martin_
Here are five gospel truths I try to preach to myself when I feel overcome with a critical spirit, whether that critical spirit is directed at my own shortcomings or another’s.

### 1. God’s judgment of me outranks my own.

> “It is of little importance to me that I should be judged by you or by any human court. In fact, I don’t even judge myself. For I am not conscious of anything against myself, but I am not justified by this. It is the Lord who judges me.”\
> [1 Corinthians 4:3-4](https://www.bible.com/bible/59/1CO.4.3-4)

> “This is how we will know that we belong to the truth and will reassure our hearts before him whenever our hearts condemn us; for God is greater than our hearts, and he knows all things.”\
> [1 John 3:19-20](https://www.bible.com/bible/59/1JN.3.19-20)

### 2. My standing rests on Christ’s performance, not my performance.

> [!Bible] [Romans 8:1 ESV](https://www.bible.com/bible/59/ROM.8.1)
> There is therefore now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus.

> [!Bible] [2 Corinthians 5:21 NLT](https://www.bible.com/bible/116/2CO.5.21)
> For God made Christ, who never sinned, to be the offering for our sin, so that we could be made right with God through Christ.

> [!Bible] [Philippians 3:8-9 ESV](https://www.bible.com/bible/59/PHP.3.8-9)
> Indeed, I count everything as loss because of the surpassing worth of knowing Christ Jesus my Lord. For his sake I have suffered the loss of all things and count them as rubbish, in order that I may gain Christ and be found in him, not having a righteousness of my own that comes from the law, but that which comes through faith in Christ, the righteousness from God that depends on faith
> [Philippians 3:8-9 NVI](https://www.bible.com/bible/128/PHP.3.8-9)

### 3. God is gentler and more gracious with me than I am of myself.

> [!Bible] [Psalm 103:13-14 ESV](https://www.bible.com/bible/59/PSA.103.13-14)
> As a father shows compassion to his children,
> so the LORD shows compassion to those who fear him.
> For he knows our frame;
> he remembers that we are dust. [Psalm 103:13-14](https://www.bible.com/bible/128/PSA.103.13-14)

> [!Bible] [Isaiah 42:3 NLT](https://www.bible.com/bible/116/ISA.42.3) [Isaiah 42:3](https://www.bible.com/bible/128/ISA.42.3)
> He will not crush the weakest reed
> or put out a flickering candle.
> He will bring justice to all who have been wronged.

[Matthew 11:28-30 NLT](https://www.bible.com/bible/116/MAT.11.28-30) [Matthew 11:28-30 NVI](https://www.bible.com/bible/128/MAT.11.28-30)

> “Come to me, all of you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest. Take up my yoke and learn from me, because I am lowly and humble in heart, and you will find rest for your souls. For my yoke is easy and my burden is light.”

### 4. Sanctification is God’s work on God’s timeline.

> [Philippians 1:6 NLT](https://www.bible.com/bible/116/PHP.1.6) [Philippians 1:6 NVI](https://www.bible.com/bible/128/PHP.1.6)
> “I am sure of this, that he who started a good work in you will carry it on to completion until the day of Christ Jesus.”

> “Therefore, my dear friends, just as you have always obeyed, so now, not only in my presence but even more in my absence, work out your own salvation with fear and trembling. For it is God who is working in you both to will and to work according to his good purpose.”\
> [Philippians 2:12-13 ESV](https://www.bible.com/bible/59/PHP.2.12-13) [Philippians 2:12-13 NVI](https://www.bible.com/bible/128/PHP.2.12-13)

> “We all, with unveiled faces, are looking as in a mirror at the glory of the Lord and are being transformed into the same image from glory to glory; this is from the Lord who is the Spirit.”\
> [2 Corinthians 3:18](https://www.bible.com/bible/116/2CO.3.18) [2 Corinthians 3:18 NVI](https://www.bible.com/bible/128/2CO.3.18)

### 5. God’s power shows up in weakness, not perfection.

> “But he said to me, 'My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is perfected in weakness.' Therefore, I will most gladly boast all the more about my weaknesses, so that Christ's power may reside in me. So I take pleasure in weaknesses, insults, hardships, persecutions, and difficulties, for the sake of Christ. For when I am weak, then I am strong.”\
> [2 Corinthians 12:9-10 NLT](https://www.bible.com/bible/116/2CO.12.9-10) [2 Corinthians 12:9-10NVI](https://www.bible.com/bible/128/2CO.12.9-10)
